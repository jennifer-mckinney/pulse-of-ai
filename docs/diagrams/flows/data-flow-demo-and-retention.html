Data flow · demo population and retention

The demo population path (fictional demo-feed posts stored and scored through the real pipeline, labelled DEMO end to end; the page's bundled fallback, never stored or scored, is separate) and the retention flows run by the worker's maintenance jobs: text removal by blanking (platform-terms windows, the Reddit deletion re-check, the detail window), the demo purge, monthly compaction and the source_runs rollup.
