State · retention lifecycle of a stored post

What becomes of a post over time: live Reddit text blanked at 48 hours or on upstream deletion, demo posts purged at the retention boundary, and real posts meant to be compacted into monthly rollups, which on master rolls back at the content-nulling step, with what is kept at each step.

  state "content nulling blocked<br/>raw_posts.content is NOT NULL,<br/>so a real month cannot finish compacting" as blocked_null
  state "scheduled compaction and<br/>per-source retention windows" as wip_sched

  detail --> blanked : Reddit only, 48 h after collection (worker, every 5 min)
  detail --> blanked : Reddit only, re-check finds it deleted or removed (every 6 h)
  detail --> purged : demo feed only, older than RETENTION_DETAIL_DAYS (90) at npm run compact
  detail --> blocked_null : real post, its month holds a post older than 90 days, npm run compact
  blocked_null --> detail : step 5 (null raw_posts.content) fails, the month's transaction rolls back rollups and embedding deletes
  blanked --> blocked_null : its month holds a post older than 90 days (same rollback)
  blocked_null --> compacted : once raw_posts.content may be NULL (not on master)
  detail --> wip_sched
  purged --> [*]
