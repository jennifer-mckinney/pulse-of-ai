State · retention lifecycle of a stored post

What becomes of a post over time: its text blanked when its source's window ends (platform terms or the detail window) or when Reddit reports it deleted, demo posts purged at the retention boundary, and real posts compacted into monthly rollups, with what is kept at each step.

  state "Tier 2 · compacted month<br/>monthly_topic_rollups + monthly_source_rollups,<br/>any remaining text blanked (never nulled),<br/>embeddings deleted; row, scores and audit rows kept" as compacted

  detail --> blanked : Reddit 48 h, YouTube and TikTok 30 days after collection (maintenance job, every 5 min)
  detail --> blanked : Reddit only, the re-check finds it deleted or removed (every 6 h, gate open)
  detail --> removed : any other real source, RETENTION_DETAIL_DAYS (90) after collection (same job)
  detail --> purged : demo feed only, older than RETENTION_DETAIL_DAYS (daily job or npm run compact)
  blanked --> compacted : its month ended before the cutoff (daily job or npm run compact)
  removed --> compacted : its month ended before the cutoff
  detail --> compacted : text still stored in a due month, blanked by compaction in the month's transaction
  purged --> [*]
