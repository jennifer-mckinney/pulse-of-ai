  divide --> negativity : scroll
  negativity --> positivity : scroll
  positivity --> drivers : scroll
  drivers --> themes_warm : scroll
  themes_warm --> themes_cold : scroll
  themes_cold --> messengers : scroll
  messengers --> summary : scroll
  summary --> explore : scroll past the explore threshold
  explore --> summary : scroll back up (exploring-changed event, false)

  overview --> explore : Skip to the globe
  note left of overview
    Any beat: a progress rail dot jumps to its beat.
    The SKIP STORY · EXPLORE pill (shown once progress
    passes 0.14, outside explore) jumps to explore.
    The header about chip toggles the about panel
    (licence, source code, legal notice; legal.config.js).
  end note

  state explore {
    direction TB
    [*] --> free
    free : globe interactive, filter panel, sorted city list, source ribbon
    free --> city : click a city, or a story-card drill chip (drill event)
    city : city detail with its posts
    city --> receipt : why? on a post
    receipt : audit drawer, 4 audiences
    receipt --> city : close
    city --> free : close
    free --> health : header health chip (also on every story beat)
    health : health drawer, system-alert banner, SYSTEM WATCHDOG, alert history, methodology, sources
    health --> free : close
  }

  note right of negativity
    Unless on the bundled fallback, CH03 and CH04 show a featured post
    whose "Why does it say that?" button opens the
    same audit drawer (PulseUI.openAudit, or the
    pulse:trace event if ui.js has not loaded)
  end note

  note right of summary
    legend: done: on master, or ships in this PR
    (the legal-notice UI)
  end note
